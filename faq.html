WaitLink offers 3 flexible pricing tiers:

  • Starter Tier (Free): $0/month forever. Full basic queue functionality for single shops.
  • Pro Business Tier ($15/month): Includes custom shop branding/logo upload, promotional date-window banners, CSV data exports, and audio chime alerts.
  • Enterprise Tier ($49/month): Includes multi-location directory management, priority SuperAdmin support, custom SLA guarantees, and advanced business intelligence analytics.

All subscription payments, upgrades, and downgrades are processed securely through Stripe Direct Checkout API. When upgrading or switching plans, Stripe automatically manages billing cycles and prorated charge credits.
