WaitLink offers 3 flexible pricing tiers:

  • Starter Tier (Free): $0/month forever. Full basic queue functionality for single shops with 7-day rolling history.
  • Pro Business Tier ($15/month): Includes custom shop branding/logo upload, promotional date-window banners, 90-day history retention with RFC 4180 CSV exports, automated 30-day PII masking, and audio chime alerts.
  • Enterprise Tier ($49/month): Includes multi-location directory management, 365-day history retention across all locations, cross-branch CSV exports, priority SuperAdmin support, custom SLA guarantees, and advanced business intelligence analytics.

Historical customer visit logs are retained based on your subscription tier: 7 days on Starter (Free), 90 days (3 months) on Pro Business, and 365 days (1 year) on Enterprise. To comply with CCPA, TCPA, and GDPR data minimization requirements, all records older than 30 days automatically have customer phone numbers (e.g., +1 (555) •••-••89) and surnames (e.g., Jane D••••) permanently anonymized across all screens and exports while preserving 100% of operational throughput metrics (wait times, turnaround speed, and completion status).

Clinical & Healthcare Privacy Mode is designed specifically for urgent care centers, walk-in clinics, outpatient facilities, and wellness practices. When enabled in Shop Settings:

  • Patient full names are automatically truncated on public lobby screens and customer mobile tickets (e.g., "Jonathan Edwards" becomes "Jonathan E.").
  • Patient phone numbers are masked by default on front desk screens with a secure staff tap-to-reveal toggle to prevent lobby shoulder-surfing.
  • Generates anonymous patient ticket IDs (e.g., "Patient #104") across queue announcements and live trackers.
  • Hides sensitive medical service descriptions from public-facing customer screens.

  • 1-Click Station Link / QR Scan: Tablet scans bypass the sign-in modal entirely, opening Front Desk instantly with zero typing.
  • Automatic URL & Device Detection: If staff open the sign-in modal on a tablet or shop URL, the Store Location is automatically pre-filled and labeled (e.g. Active: Stomada Clinic).
  • Smart Name Matching: If staff ever type manually, they can type either their business name (e.g. "Stomada Clinic") or slug, or pick from the 1-tap autocomplete datalist.
Why is a shop scope needed behind the scenes? In a cloud multi-tenant architecture with thousands of shops, multiple stores employ staff named "reception" or "sarah". Scoping to the shop protects privacy and prevents cross-store account collisions while maintaining zero friction for staff.
