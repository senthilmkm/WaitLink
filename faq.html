Yes. WaitLink connects to an enterprise Twilio SMS gateway. When staff tap "SERVE NOW", an automated text message is dispatched to the customer's mobile number. The SMS contains their direct ticket tracking link (e.g., https://waitlink.app?s=yourshop&view=public&ticket=id), allowing customers to see their live visit status, service details, and greeting instructions without downloading any mobile app. Shop owners can also customize the message wording and template tags in Shop Settings.

The Pro Business plan ($15/month) includes 500 automated SMS carrier segments per month. If you do not consume all 500 SMS during your billing cycle, WaitLink's automated 1-Month Rollover Bank protects your unused included balance and rolls it over into your next month (up to a 1,000 SMS reserve limit). This ensures you never lose credits you paid for during quieter business periods.

WaitLink guarantees zero disruption to your shop's operations. If your SMS credit balance reaches zero, front desk queue calling, wait time timers, and public lobby screens continue operating 100% normally without any blocking popups. Only outbound text message dispatches are paused. Shop owners can top up instantly in Owner Settings by purchasing one-time packs: Micro (+250 SMS for $5.00), Growth (+1,000 SMS for $15.00), or Power (+2,500 SMS for $39.00). All top-up packs feature a Lifetime Non-Expiration Guarantee and remain in your reserve wallet forever until fully used.

WaitLink offers 3 flexible pricing tiers:

  • Starter Tier (Free): $0/month forever. Full basic queue functionality for single shops with 7-day rolling history.
  • Pro Business Tier ($15/month): Includes custom shop branding/logo upload, promotional date-window banners, 90-day history retention with RFC 4180 CSV exports, automated 30-day PII masking, and audio chime alerts.
  • Enterprise Tier ($49/month): Includes multi-location directory management, 365-day history retention across all locations, cross-branch CSV exports, priority SuperAdmin support, custom SLA guarantees, and advanced business intelligence analytics.

Historical customer visit logs are retained based on your subscription tier: 7 days on Starter (Free), 90 days (3 months) on Pro Business, and 365 days (1 year) on Enterprise. To comply with CCPA, TCPA, and GDPR data minimization requirements, all records older than 30 days automatically have customer phone numbers (e.g., +1 (555) •••-••89) and surnames (e.g., Jane D••••) permanently anonymized across all screens and exports while preserving 100% of operational throughput metrics (wait times, turnaround speed, and completion status).

WaitLink enforces strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) to keep customer queues seamless while protecting business financials:

  • 👤 Guest User (Walk-in Customer): Zero account or app required. Joins via window QR poster or lobby kiosk. Receives an interactive digital ticket with real-time wait estimation and optional turn-ready SMS alerts. Can cancel or leave the line anytime.
  • 🖥️ Front Desk User (Counter Staff / Operator): Accesses front desk queue control via 1-Click Station Link, iPad QR, or counter PIN passcode. Can call next ("SERVE NEXT"), seat customers, mark visits complete or no-show, adjust wait times, and manually add walk-in guests. Strictly restricted from billing, subscription upgrades, SMS wallet purchasing, and shop deletion.
  • 👑 Shop Owner (Business Account Admin): Authenticated via Google or Email. Full business sovereignty: manages storefront hours and services, provisions staff accounts and individual PINs, oversees Stripe billing and non-expiring SMS top-ups, configures data retention & HIPAA privacy policies, and exports 90-day/365-day CSV history logs.
  • ⚡ SuperAdmin (Platform Operator): System-wide infrastructure monitoring, revenue metrics, global compliance audits, and multi-tenant tenant isolation.

When a walk-in customer scans your lobby QR poster, their phone opens their personal live digital ticket. They can see their position in line (e.g., "2 ahead of you"), real-time ticking wait time calculated from your shop's recent service velocity, and their selected service.
