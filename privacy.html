Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 16, 2026 • Version 2.5

1. Zero-Friction Customer Privacy Mandate

WaitLink is built on a strict privacy-first principle: walk-in customers checking into a line are never required to register accounts, enter passwords, or provide sensitive personal data. Customer check-in names or initials are used solely for identifying line position during active visits.

2. Information We Collect & Why Phone Numbers Are Collected

For Business Owners: When you set up a shop account, we store your email address, business name, custom URL slug, shop address/hours, optional logo image, and selected subscription tier.

For Walk-in Customers: We temporarily store transient check-in names (e.g. "Marcus S.") and optional mobile cell phone numbers if voluntarily provided. Why we collect phone numbers: Customer phone numbers are collected strictly and solely to deliver operational, real-time SMS text notifications when your turn at a business chair or table is ready. Daily visit history logs can be reset by the shop owner at any time.

📱 3. SMS Text Notification Privacy & Mobile Data Disclaimer

WaitLink integrates with carrier-authorized SMS gateways (Twilio) to send operational line position text messages. We strictly enforce CTIA and TCPA privacy standards:

  • Zero Third-Party Data Sharing: Mobile subscriber information and SMS opt-in data will NOT be shared, sold, rented, or leased to any third parties, affiliates, or advertising networks for marketing or promotional purposes under any circumstances.
  • Purpose-Specific Use Only: Phone numbers are never used for promotional marketing campaigns, phone calls, or unsolicited text messages.
  • Opt-In Consent: Entering your cell phone number into a WaitLink check-in form constitutes voluntary consent to receive transactional queue status text messages for that visit.
  • Opt-Out & Help: You may opt out of receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP or CANCEL to any text message. Reply HELP for customer support.
  • Message Rates & Frequency: Message frequency varies per queue visit (typically 1 to 2 transactional messages per check-in). Standard message and data rates from your mobile carrier may apply.

🏥 4. Clinical & Healthcare Privacy Mode (Urgent Care, Walk-in Clinics & Wellness)

WaitLink includes an advanced Clinical & Healthcare Privacy Mode purpose-built for urgent care centers, medical walk-ins, outpatient clinics, and wellness practices where patient confidentiality is paramount:

  • Public Screen Patient Name Masking: Full patient names are automatically masked on public lobby monitors and customer mobile cards (e.g., "Jonathan Edwards" is displayed as "Jonathan E.").
  • Front Desk Phone Protection with Tap-to-Reveal: Patient phone numbers are masked by default on front desk terminals (e.g., +1 (•••) •••-1359). Front desk receptionists can tap a secure toggle to temporarily reveal the number when direct verification is required, preventing lobby shoulder-surfing.
  • Anonymous Patient Ticket IDs: When enabled, ticket tracking automatically uses anonymous patient codes (e.g., "Patient #104") rather than public identifiers across queue announcements and live status views.
  • Service Name Concealment: Sensitive healthcare and medical appointment service descriptions are strictly hidden from public-facing customer screens, ensuring waiting patients cannot view other individuals' medical care categories.

🛡️ 5. Queue History Data Retention, Privacy Auto-Masking & CSV Reporting

In accordance with data minimization standards under CCPA, TCPA, and GDPR, WaitLink enforces automated queue history lifecycle policies governed centrally by Platform Administrators:

  • Tier-Governed Retention Windows: Queue history logs are retained based on your subscription tier:
    • Starter (Free): 7 days of rolling queue history.
    • Pro Business ($15/mo): 90 days (3 months) of rolling queue history with full CSV audit export.
    • Enterprise ($49/mo): 365 days (1 year) of rolling history across all regional clinic and shop branches.
  • Automated 30-Day PII Auto-Masking: For all visits completed more than 30 days prior, customer Personally Identifiable Information (PII) is permanently anonymized:
    • Phone numbers are automatically masked as +1 (555) •••-••89.
    • Customer last names are redacted as Jane D••••.
    • Masked records display an immutable 🛡️ MASKED compliance tag in all audit tables.
  • Preservation of Operational Metrics: 100% of aggregate turnaround metrics—including wait duration, service time, arrival hour, and visit disposition (completed vs. cancelled)—are preserved permanently for historical business intelligence without storing unneeded personal data.
  • Tier-Gated RFC 4180 CSV Reporting: Exported history reports use RFC 4180 CSV encoding with UTF-8 BOM (\uFEFF) for full Excel and Google Sheets fidelity, automatically applying PII masking rules to safeguard exported data files.

6. Stripe Payment Security

All subscription transactions for Pro Business ($15/mo) and Enterprise ($49/mo) plans are handled securely by Stripe (PCI-DSS Level 1 Compliant). WaitLink servers never store or process raw credit card numbers or billing CVC codes.

7. Data Encryption & Storage

All data transmitted between web browsers, front desk tablets, and Google Cloud Infrastructure is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and encrypted at rest with AES-256 industry encryption standards.

8. LocalStorage & Cookies

WaitLink uses HTML5 LocalStorage to maintain active shop preferences, temporary customer ticket timers, and sound notification settings locally on your device. We do not sell user data to third-party ad networks.

If you have questions about your business data, privacy rights, or data deletion requests, contact our Data Protection Officer directly at senthil930@gmail.com.
