How JumpIn Works

JumpIn is a free community carpool board — no app download, no account signup, and no app fees.

Browse & Post Rides

For Drivers — Share Empty Seats in 3 Steps

1
Post your trip

Enter your name, mobile number, route, departure time, available seats, and optional per-seat ride cost or pickup note.

2
Share your trip link

Copy your unique trip link or share it directly in WhatsApp so community members can view live seat availability.

3
Manage via SMS

Get an instant SMS link to add passengers, message riders with quick updates, or mark your trip complete.

For Riders — Reserve Seats in 3 Steps

1
Filter & pick a ride

Browse upcoming trips by date (Today, Tomorrow, This week) or search by route, city, driver, or Trip ID.

2
Review & reserve

Tap a trip card to review departure time, pickup note, and seat count, then tap Reserve a seat to book 1 or more seats.

3
Stay updated

Receive an SMS link to adjust your seat count, message the driver, or cancel anytime if your plans change.
