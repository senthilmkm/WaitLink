Understanding SMS Carrier Segments & Credits

How character length and emojis impact your SMS billing

💡 Why are SMS alerts measured in "Segments"? Telecommunication carriers (Verizon, AT&T, T-Mobile, Twilio) do not bill by single message. They bill per Carrier Segment. If your message exceeds 160 characters, cellular networks automatically split and concatenate it into multiple segments, debiting 1 credit per segment.
📝 GSM-7 Standard Text Recommended

Standard English letters, numbers, and basic punctuation.

• 1 Segment (1 Credit): Up to 160 chars
• 2 Segments (2 Credits): 161 – 306 chars
• 3 Segments (3 Credits): 307 – 459 chars
*Multi-segment GSM uses 153 chars/segment due to 7-byte carrier concatenation headers.
⚠️ UCS-2 Unicode / Emojis Consumes 2× Credits

Any message containing emojis (💈, ✂️, 🕒) or accented non-GSM characters.

• 1 Segment (1 Credit): Up to 70 chars only
• 2 Segments (2 Credits): 71 – 134 chars
• 3 Segments (3 Credits): 135 – 201 chars
*Carriers drop segment capacity to 70 chars whenever a 16-bit Unicode character is detected.
🎯 Recommended Template for 1-Credit Delivery: Hi {name}! Your turn at {shop} is ready now. Your ticket: {link}
This template stays under 160 characters, ensuring each customer alert uses exactly 1 credit out of your monthly quota.
