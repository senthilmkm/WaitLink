Community Safety & Carpool Etiquette

Simple guidelines to keep every shared ride smooth, respectful, and reliable.

For Drivers

  • Keep details accurate: Double-check departure time, pickup spot, available seats, and any suggested per-seat cost before posting.
  • Communicate early: Use quick trip updates if you are running late or changing pickup landmarks.
  • Drive legally & safely: Maintain a valid driver's license, active auto insurance, and a roadworthy vehicle.

For Riders

  • Reserve only what you need: Book the exact seat count for your party and add an optional drop-off note if helpful.
  • Be on time: Arrive at the pickup spot a few minutes early and use your SMS link if plans change.
  • Settle costs transparently: If a trip displays a suggested ride cost, confirm and settle directly offline with your driver.

Community Moderation & Privacy

  • Protected phone numbers: Full phone numbers are never displayed publicly on the rides board.
  • Admin oversight: Community administrators can enable Members-Only access, require trip approval, or block disruptive phone numbers.
  • Need help? Reach out via our Contact page and include the Trip ID if applicable.
