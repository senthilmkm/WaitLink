JumpIn Privacy Policy

Effective: November 2026 • WaitLink JumpIn Community Carpool

JumpIn is designed to coordinate community rides with minimal personal data and zero user accounts or passwords.

1. Information We Collect

  • Trip & Seat Details: Display name, US/Canada mobile phone number, route (From / To), departure date and time, estimated trip duration, seat count, and optional pickup notes.
  • Trip Messages: Updates you post inside a trip's message thread.
  • Device Tokens: A cryptographic management secret stored locally in your browser (localStorage) so you can manage your trip or seat without logging in.

2. How Phone Numbers Are Protected

  • Phone numbers are stored only in private server-side collections and are never published on public ride boards or ride pages.
  • In the JumpIn Admin console, phone numbers are masked to the last 4 digits (e.g., ····5678).
  • Recovery link requests always return a neutral response so unauthorized visitors cannot check whether a phone number is on a trip.

3. SMS Text Messages & Opt-Out

JumpIn uses Twilio to send transactional SMS alerts solely for ride coordination (manage links, trip updates, cancellations, and optional post-ride thank-you notices). You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any JumpIn text message.

4. Automatic Data Retention & Deletion

  • 7-Day Private Purge: Private trip records, reservations, and phone numbers are automatically deleted 7 days after a trip is completed, cancelled, or declined.
  • 30-Day Public Purge: Remaining public trip stubs are deleted 30 days after purge.

5. Contact

For privacy questions or data removal requests, email senthil930@gmail.com or visit JumpIn Contact.
