Answers to Common Business Owner Questions

Quick responses to handle the most common merchant questions with confidence.

📋 "We already use a paper clipboard sign-in sheet."

Paper sheets take names, but they can't tell a customer how long the wait is or text them when it's their turn. When people see 6 names on a clipboard with no estimate, they walk away. With WaitLink, they scan the QR, grab a coffee next door, and you keep the sale.

👴 "Our customers are older and might not be tech-savvy."

WaitLink requires zero app downloads. If someone doesn't want to use their phone, your front desk can type their name into the tablet in 5 seconds just like a notepad. But for everyone else, scanning the QR code keeps your lobby calm and organized.

🖥️ "Do I need to buy expensive iPad stands or hardware?"

Zero hardware required. WaitLink runs directly in the browser of any iPad, Android tablet, smartphone, or desktop computer you already have on your counter. You can print the window QR poster on any standard home or office printer.

🔒 "Can my staff accidentally see my revenue or change billing?"

Never. Staff log in with a dedicated 4-digit Front Desk PIN. They only see queue controls (Check-In, Call, Complete). Financial reports, subscription settings, and billing portals are strictly locked behind your owner master password.
