Common Requests

  • Request Members-Only Access: Email your full name, 10-digit US/Canada mobile number, and which community group you are joining (e.g., Its-OK or your neighborhood group name).
  • Start a Private Neighborhood Group: Want an isolated board for your HOA, school, or community? Sign in with Google at jumpin-admin.html?tab=groups to request one.
  • Lost Manage Link: Open the trip page on waitlink.app/?s=jumpin (or your group’s ?g= link) and tap Driver? Manage this trip or Rider? Manage seat or message driver to receive a fresh SMS link.
  • SMS Opt-Out: Reply STOP to any JumpIn text message to stop receiving SMS alerts immediately.
  • Report Abuse or Safety Concern: Email senthil930@gmail.com with the group name, trip route, and approximate departure time.
