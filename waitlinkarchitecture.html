Purpose of WaitLink & The Problem Solved

⚠️ The Walk-Away Revenue Loss Crisis

Walk-in service businesses (barbershops, urgent care clinics, pet groomers, auto service bays, DMV offices) lose up to 30% of peak rush revenue simply because prospective customers look at a crowded waiting room and walk away without ever checking in.

Traditional solutions fail because they require costly iPad kiosks ($800+), cumbersome native app downloads with accounts and passwords, or expensive enterprise contracts with $300/mo minimums.

💡 The Zero-Barrier WaitLink Solution

WaitLink replaces physical buzzers and kiosks with a simple, high-resolution printable Window QR Poster. Customers scan the flyer with their native mobile phone camera, enter their name and optional mobile number in 2 seconds, and immediately track their live spot in line with a real-time ticking clock.

When their turn is ready, front desk staff tap "SERVE NOW", and WaitLink automatically dispatches an instant transactional SMS with their direct live ticket link, enabling customers to step outside, grab coffee, and return exactly when called.

Process Flows by Role (End-to-End)

👤 1. Customer End-User Journey

Step 1: Scan Arrive & Point Camera

Customer approaches storefront and points mobile phone camera at the WaitLink Window Poster QR code. Opens directly in mobile Safari/Chrome.

Step 2: Check-In 2-Second Self Check-In

Types name/initials, chooses desired service chip (e.g. Haircut 20m), and enters optional mobile number with CTIA/TCPA SMS consent disclosure.

Step 3: Track Live Dynamic Ticket

Receives a personal live ticket showing real-time ticking wait timer, position in line (e.g., #3 in line), and a "🕒 Running Late" action button.

Step 4: Turn Ready SMS Delivered & Visit In Progress

When desk calls, receives instant SMS with live ticket link. Ticket updates to Visit In Progress with quick +5m / +10m Running Late grace chips to alert staff if delayed parking, preventing premature No-Show marks.

💈 2. Front Desk Staff Workflow & Station RBAC

Step 1: Provision Role Title & Permission Presets

Owner provisions account with assigned Role Title (Front Desk Receptionist, Intake Greeter / Host, Service Caller / Stylist) and applies 1-tap permission presets governing check-in, calling, phone reveal, and history.

Step 2: Launch 1-Click Station URL & iPad QR

Staff scan the on-screen Station QR with an iPad camera or open their bookmarked 1-Click URL (?s=slug&staff=user&key=pin). Station instantly unlocks Front Desk with zero typing. Add to Home Screen creates a full-screen counter kiosk.

Step 3: Shift Swap Active Role Pill & PIN Switcher

Front Desk header displays active operator name + assigned role title chip (e.g. [Front Desk Receptionist]). Tapping the chip triggers a 2-second PIN switcher (promptStaffPinSwitch()) for seamless operator handovers.

Step 4: Boundary Strict Role Isolation & Lockouts

Governed strictly by hasStaffPermission(). Check-in, calling, and phone unmasking are gated. Staff are permanently locked out of business settings, Stripe billing, and shop deletion.

💡 Architecture Justification: Staff Sign-In & Store Location Slug

Why Multi-Tenancy Scopes Staff by Shop Slug: WaitLink hosts thousands of independent storefronts. Multiple shops routinely employ staff named "reception" or "sarah" with standard 4-digit PINs. Scoping staff roles to /shops/{slug}/staff_roles guarantees absolute tenant isolation, prevents cross-shop username collisions, and allows counter operators to use clean, simple usernames without forced global prefixes.

1. Automatic Detection & Pre-Fill:
When opening the app at a store URL or on a previously signed-in tablet, the Store Location is auto-detected from active state or localStorage. Staff never have to type the slug.
2. Datalist & Name Fallback:
A 1-tap suggestion datalist is provided. If staff type the human business name (e.g. "Stomada Clinic"), smart query resolution matches the shop document automatically.
3. 1-Click Station Links / QR Bypass:
Front desk iPads using the 1-Click Station URL or scanning the Station QR bypass credentials entirely, unlocking Front Desk in under 1 second with 0 keystrokes.

🧱 Two-Tier Storage Architecture & 1MB Document Boundary Safeguard

High-volume storefronts accumulate tens of thousands of customer visits per year. A monolithic array inside a single Firestore document hits Cloud Firestore's 1 MB document limit at ~2,500 records. WaitLink resolves this with an active cache + partitioned subcollection pattern:

Tier 1: In-Memory / Document Cache

Recent History Buffer

The most recent completed visits reside inside the primary shop document (capped at 200 items). This enables instant < 50ms UI loads when operators open the Front Desk or Visit History logs, with zero secondary network lookups.

Tier 2: Partitioned Subcollection

/shops/{slug}/history/{ticketId}

Every completed, cancelled, or no-show customer visit is atomically synchronized to a discrete subcollection document. This eliminates the 1MB ceiling entirely, scaling to hundreds of thousands of historical records without degrading shop document performance.

Client-Side Lazy Reconciliation

State Reconciliation & Cleanup

During live sync and owner view activation, performClientSideLazyCleanup() prunes expired records beyond tier retention windows (7d / 90d / 365d) while maintaining seamless pagination and zero disruption to active line callers.

🛡️ Automated PII Anonymization & Data Sanitization Pipeline

To comply with CCPA, TCPA, and GDPR data minimization requirements, customer personal information is subject to a strict 30-day anonymization boundary governed centrally via /settings/retention_config:

📱 Phone Number Masking

Regex pipeline maskHistoricalPhone(phone) preserves standard country prefix and terminal digits while redacting the exchange:

"+1 (555) 234-5689" → "+1 (555) •••-••89"

👤 Customer Name Masking

String tokenizer maskHistoricalName(name) preserves first name or initials while irrevocably masking family surnames:

"Jonathan Edwards" → "Jonathan E••••"

📈 Metric Integrity Safeguard

Anonymization operates non-destructively on timing metrics. Throughput analytics retain 100% precision:

{ waitMins: 14, serviceMins: 22, status: "completed" }

🏥 Clinical & Healthcare Privacy Mode System Architecture

Urgent care walk-in clinics, outpatient centers, and wellness practices operate under strict HIPAA confidentiality mandates. When clinicalPrivacyMode: true is activated in shop settings:

  • Public Screen Isolation: Public TV monitors and mobile customer web cards strictly truncate full patient names to first name and last initial (e.g. "Jonathan E.").
  • Front Desk Tap-to-Reveal Phone Protection: Front desk tablets mask patient phone numbers by default (e.g. +1 (•••) •••-1359). Receptionists tap a secure badge to reveal the number for 10 seconds during patient check-in or telephone verification.
  • Anonymous Patient Ticket Identifiers: Ticket numbers render as anonymous health codes (e.g. "Patient #104"), avoiding public exposure of patron identities.
  • Medical Service Concealment: Specific medical consultation and clinical treatment service tags are suppressed on public customer views, keeping health visit categories private.

📲 SMS Outbound Volume Quotas, Metering & Top-Up Engine

Automated customer SMS notifications are a primary driver of walk-away elimination, but unmetered carrier dispatches introduce uncapped telecom COGS risk. WaitLink deploys a rigorous 3-phase metering and quota governance architecture that protects platform margins while delivering a flawless, zero-disruption front desk experience:

Carrier Segment Accounting

GSM-7 vs. UCS-2 Metering

Telecommunication carriers bill messages by segments. Standard alphanumeric text uses GSM 03.38 (160 characters/segment). Including even one emoji or special symbol triggers UCS-2 Unicode encoding (70 characters/segment). WaitLink's live template meter displays exact characters and segments in real time before staff save templates.

Zero-Disruption Principle

Front Desk Uninterrupted Calling

If a shop exhausts its SMS credit balance (0 credits remaining), front desk queue calling, wait time clocks, and lobby screen displays proceed 100% normally. Only outbound text dispatches are paused with a subtle amber banner. Receptionists are never blocked by disruptive modal popups.

Rollover Governance

Option A vs. Option B Toggle

Platform SuperAdmins control renewal mechanics via /settings/pricing_config.rolloverPolicy:
• Option A (Monthly Reset): Unused included quota expires at billing renewal; refreshes strictly to 500.
• Option B (1-Month Rollover Bank): Unused included SMS roll over for 1 month (capped at 1,000 SMS reserve).

Security Architecture & Rules Enforcement

🛡️ Firestore Security Rules Isolation

WaitLink eliminates open wildcard access. Access is strictly constrained by authentication state and role:

  • No Open Scraping: Direct allow list queries across the entire /shops collection are blocked for unauthenticated users.
  • Customer Check-In Safeguard: Unauthenticated customer check-ins are restricted to updating only the queue and stats fields via affectedKeys().hasOnly(...). Customers cannot overwrite owner email, settings, or plans.
  • History Subcollection Partitioning: The /shops/{shopId}/history/{ticketId} subcollection restricts read, write, and delete permissions exclusively to authenticated shop owners (isOwner(shopId)) and Platform SuperAdmins (isSuperAdmin()). Public customer queries are rejected.
  • Retention Policy Governance: The centralized /settings/retention_config document permits public read for client-side policy synchronization, but write mutations are strictly locked to verified SuperAdmins.
  • SMS & Inquiry Protection: Outbound SMS and contact inquiry tables are blocked from public reads.

🔑 Git & Secrets Management Architecture

Credentials and operational API keys are permanently decoupled from Git:

  • config.secrets.js holds local credentials and is excluded via .gitignore and firebase.json.
  • config.secrets.example.js is tracked in Git as a placeholder template.
  • Source files (app.js, index.html) use the dynamic window.WAITLINK_SECRETS loader with zero hardcoded secret strings.

🔒 3. Triple-Layer Anti-Abuse Architecture: Sealing "Forever-Free" Loopholes

WaitLink uses a defense-in-depth model to ensure malicious or clever users cannot bypass paywalls, forge subscription plans, or spin up multiple shops indefinitely for free:

Layer 1: Presentation & UI Gating
Context-Aware Elements

renderSubscriptionBadges() hides the header & drawer Upgrade buttons for all guest visitors. updateCreateShopButtonsVisibility() automatically hides all "+ Create Shop" buttons on desktop and mobile once an owner registers 1 shop, replacing the dropdown action with an Enterprise upsell chip.

Layer 2: Client State & Cross-Tenant Barrier
Controller Paywall Checks

openCreateShopModal() and submitCreateShop() strictly check ownedShops.length >= 1. If a Starter or Pro user attempts to register a second shop, execution halts immediately and routes to the Enterprise upgrade modal. switchView() blocks unauthorized users from viewing another tenant's front desk.

Layer 3: Cryptographic Backend & Firestore Rules
Zero-Trust Cloud Authority

Even if an attacker modifies client JavaScript:
• Stripe Upgrade (verifyStripeSessionAndUpgrade) cryptographically queries the Stripe REST API server-to-server and verifies payment before updating tiers.
• Twilio SMS (sendTwilioSms) rejects dispatch if shop planPrice < 15.
• Firestore Rules disallow client SDK writes to planPrice, planTitle, or Stripe fields.

🧹 4. SuperAdmin Multi-Tenant Ghost Document Cleanup Engine

In Google Cloud Firestore, documents are keyed by their Document ID (doc.id), which can differ from an internal slug field (e.g., legacy test documents created with spaces or un-slugified IDs such as "Amy Dental clinic" or "stomada").

The updated cleanDuplicateShops() engine identifies duplicates by grouping normalized (shopName + ownerEmail) pairs, picks the canonical kebab-case document to preserve, and purges ghost documents using their exact Firestore Document ID (d.ghost.id). This permanently purges obsolete cloud documents and prevents ghost shops from re-appearing after session logout or page reload.

SuperAdmin Revenue Metrics & Account 360° Intelligence Engine

The Revenue Metrics & Account 360° engine transforms the SuperAdmin console from a flat storefront database into an owner-centric SaaS financial and operations intelligence platform. It rolls up multi-location portfolios under verified account owners, calculates platform unit economics (MRR, ARR, ARPU, ARPPU, Freemium Conversion, Top-Up Gross Margins, and LTV) with 100% mathematical precision, surfaces automated growth and churn signals, and provides a slide-out 360° account inspection drawer with immediate founder actions.

🏢 1. The Architectural Shift: Shop-Centric vs. Account-Centric Intelligence

Legacy Paradigm: Flat Shop-Centric Isolated Tenant Rows
  • Each shop was an isolated row in the database, obscuring business owners running 3 to 10 locations.
  • Zero aggregation of multi-store monthly subscription revenue (MRR) or annualized value (ARR).
  • Unable to detect if a high-volume Starter shop was prime for a Pro upsell or if an Enterprise customer had gone dormant.
  • Required manual SQL/Firestore dumps or spreadsheet wrangling to determine platform ARPU or churn rates.
Modern Paradigm: Account 360° Centric Unified Owner Portfolios
  • Automatically groups all storefronts under the verified registered ownerEmail.
  • Real-time rollup of total locations, active stores, combined MRR, foot-traffic, and reserve SMS wallets.
  • Automated heuristic state machines: surfaces 🔥 Ready for Pro, ⭐ Multi-Unit Bundle, and ⚠️ Churn Risk.
  • Slide-out 360° Drawer enables instant front desk terminal inspection, founder email outreach, and courtesy credits.
