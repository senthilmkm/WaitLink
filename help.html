📚 HELP CENTER & GUIDES

Master Your Walk-In Front Desk

Everything you need to set up your shop, print your window poster, call customers, and analyze lobby traffic.

⚡

1. Setting Up Your Shop in 10s

Click ⚡ + Create Shop in the top header menu. Enter your business name and custom URL slug (e.g. main-st-barbers). Click confirm to launch your cloud-synced waitlist immediately.

🖼️

2. Printing Your Window Poster

Navigate to Account Dropdown ▾ → 𖠁 Window QR Poster. Click Print Poster or Save as PDF. Tape the high-resolution 8.5" x 11" poster to your shop entrance window.

📲

3. Zero-Friction Customer Check-In

Walk-in customers point their mobile phone camera at your window poster QR code. They type their name/initials and instantly see their real-time line position—no app downloads required.

📞

4. 1-Tap Front Desk Control & Automated SMS

Keep the Front Desk console active on your tablet, iPad, PC, or smartphone. When a chair opens up, tap "SERVE NOW" or "SERVE NEXT". The queue updates instantaneously across all staff monitors and customer screens. If the customer provided a mobile phone number, WaitLink automatically dispatches an SMS text message to their device with a direct link to their live digital ticket.

📊

5. Business Owner Analytics

Switch to Business Owner Analytics to monitor Walk-Away Loss % (cancellations), Peak Rush Windows, Staff Throughput Speed (visits/hr), and export CSV visit logs for accounting.

💳

6. Managing Stripe Subscriptions

Upgrade to Pro Business ($15/mo) or Enterprise ($49/mo) via Stripe Direct Checkout. You can manage, cancel, or switch your active plan anytime from your user profile menu.
