1. THE OUTREACH STRATEGY & PSYCHOLOGY
================================================================================

The formula uses a 4-step consultative approach:
  1. Genuine Praise: Open with their hard-earned Google / social rating (e.g., 4.9★).
  2. The Invisible Walk-Away Bleed: Diplomatically point out that long, blind
     waits cause 20-30% of walk-ins to turn around and leave for competitors.
  3. The WaitLink Solution: Highlight zero hardware costs, QR phone scans, and
     calm lobbies.
  4. The Zero-Pressure Exit Ramp: Explicitly tell them they can ignore the note
     if they are already 100% satisfied. This lowers sales resistance dramatically.

OBJECTION 1: "We already use a clipboard / paper sign-in sheet."
ANSWER:
"Paper sign-in sheets work for taking names, but they can't tell a customer how long the wait is or text them when they are next. When people see 6 names on a clipboard, they often leave. With WaitLink, they can scan the QR code and go grab a coffee while keeping their spot. You keep the sale instead of losing them to the street."

ANSWER:
"That's the beauty of WaitLink — customers don't have to download any app or make an account. If someone doesn't want to use their phone, your receptionist simply checks them in on the front desk screen just like a notepad. But for everyone else, scanning the QR code keeps your lobby peaceful and organized."

OBJECTION 3: "Is there a long-term contract or hardware to buy?"
ANSWER:
"Zero contracts and zero hardware to buy. It runs directly on any tablet, iPad, phone, or laptop you already have at your reception desk. You can use the Starter plan completely free, and Pro is just $15/month with no cancellation fees."

OBJECTION 4: "Can my staff accidentally change my subscription or settings?"
ANSWER:
"No. Front desk staff log in using a 4-digit PIN or 1-click station link that only gives them operator controls (Check-in, Call, Cancel). Billing, plan upgrades, and owner analytics are locked behind your owner master login."
