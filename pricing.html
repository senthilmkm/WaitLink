FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

SMS Quotas & Billing Questions

📱 How many automated SMS messages are included with Pro Business?

Pro Business ($15/month) includes 500 carrier SMS segments per month at no additional cost. When staff tap "SERVE NOW" on the front desk, an automated turn-ready text message with the customer's live ticket link is instantly delivered to their phone.

🎁 What happens to unused SMS credits at the end of the month?

WaitLink features an automated 1-Month Rollover Bank. Unused included SMS credits automatically roll over into your subsequent month (up to a 1,000 SMS reserve ceiling). This eliminates "use-it-or-lose-it" frustration and guarantees maximum value.

⚡ What happens if our shop runs out of SMS credits during a busy day?

WaitLink strictly adheres to the Zero-Disruption Principle. Your front desk calling queue, wait time calculators, and public lobby displays continue operating 100% normally. Queue visits proceed uninterrupted with a discreet amber notice, and the shop owner can purchase a one-time top-up pack in seconds from Settings.

🔒 Do purchased Top-Up SMS packs ever expire?

No, never! All top-up packs ($5 for 250 SMS, $15 for 1,000 SMS, and $39 for 2,500 SMS) come with a Lifetime Non-Expiration Guarantee. They are stored in your shop's dedicated reserve wallet and remain active indefinitely until 100% consumed.
