JumpIn Terms of Use

Effective: November 2026 • WaitLink JumpIn Community Carpool

By posting a trip, reserving a seat, or using JumpIn, you agree to these Terms of Use.

1. Free Community Coordination Tool

JumpIn is a free community carpool coordination board provided by WaitLink. WaitLink does not provide transportation services, employ drivers, inspect vehicles, or verify driver licenses or insurance. All rides are voluntary, peer-to-peer arrangements between community members.

2. No Payments Processed

WaitLink does not charge booking fees or process ride fares. Any optional community tip (such as Zelle or Venmo) shown on JumpIn is strictly voluntary to help community organizers cover SMS delivery costs.

3. Driver & Rider Responsibilities

  • Drivers are responsible for maintaining a valid driver's license, active auto insurance, safe vehicle operation, and accurate trip details.
  • Riders are responsible for arriving on time, reserving only the seats they need, and updating or cancelling their reservation before departure if plans change.
  • Respectful Conduct: Do not post false trips, reserve seats you do not intend to use, or send spam through trip messages.

4. SMS Consent

By entering your mobile phone number to post a trip or reserve a seat, you consent to receive transactional SMS messages related to that trip. Standard carrier message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out.

5. Community Moderation & Limits

Community administrators may enable Members-Only access, require trip approval, limit daily posts or seats, cancel trips, or block phone numbers to protect the community.
