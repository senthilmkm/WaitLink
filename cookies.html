🍪 STORAGE POLICY

Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Last Updated: October 18, 2026 • Version 2.6

1. Zero Third-Party Advertising or Tracking Cookies

WaitLink operates on a transparent, privacy-first model: we do NOT deploy third-party advertising cookies, ad beacons, retargeting pixels, or behavioral tracking cookies. We never sell or license customer interaction data to external data brokers or advertisers.

🛡️ 2. Cross-Browser SafeStorage Engine & In-Memory Fallback

WaitLink features a resilient SafeStorage abstraction layer engineered to work reliably across all browser privacy configurations, including Microsoft Edge InPrivate, Apple Safari Private Browsing, Google Chrome Incognito, and Firefox Private Windows:

  • Graceful Private Mode Fallback: When browser sandbox policies or enterprise security filters block or throw quota exceptions on standard HTML5 localStorage, WaitLink seamlessly pivots to an isolated in-memory RAM store.
  • Automatic Zero-Trace Cleanup: In private browsing modes, all ticket state, authentication tokens, and preferences exist purely in transient volatile memory. Closing the browser tab instantly and irreversibly destroys this data with zero artifacts left on disk.
  • No Crashes or Frozen UIs: By preventing DOMException: QuotaExceededError or security access denials, guests and front desk operators experience uninterrupted, real-time queue synchronization even in high-security restricted environments.

3. Functional Storage Keys Inventory

WaitLink uses client-side storage solely for strictly necessary technical operations, organized below by user role:

Guest User (Walk-in Customer)

  • waitlink_ticket_{shopId}: Stores temporary ticket ID and security token so guests can refresh their mobile ticket page or reopen it without losing queue position.
  • waitlink_theme: Remembers light/dark theme preference for comfortable reading outdoors or in waiting rooms.

Front Desk User (Staff / Receptionist)

  • waitlink_staff_session: Remembers active station PIN authentication so counter staff do not have to re-enter their PIN after refreshing the queue screen.
  • waitlink_audio_enabled: Remembers front desk sound alert preference (bell/chime when new guests check in).

Shop Owner (Administrator)

  • waitlink_session_user: Stores encrypted session credentials for store administrative management.
  • waitlink_config: Caches active shop metadata, service options, and turnaround time baselines for fast offline-tolerant rendering.
  • waitlink_my_shops: Maintains a list of the owner's managed shops for instantaneous multi-location switching in the app header.

4. Managing & Clearing Your Storage

Because WaitLink does not track you across other websites, you can safely clear your local data at any time via your browser's Developer Tools or Settings ("Clear Browsing Data" → "Cookies and site data"). Furthermore, tapping "Leave Queue" on a guest ticket immediately removes your transient ticket credentials.
