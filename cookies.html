🍪 STORAGE POLICY

Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Updated: October 3, 2026

1. No Invasive Third-Party Tracking Cookies

WaitLink does not use 3rd-party advertising tracking cookies. We believe in strict data privacy for walk-in shop customers and business owners.

2. Functional LocalStorage Usage

We use standard HTML5 LocalStorage and SessionStorage strictly for functional operations:

  • waitlink_config: Stores active shop name and turnaround baseline preference.
  • waitlink_my_shops: Stores list of your created and visited shops for 1-tap header switching.
  • waitlink_session_user: Stores your active authentication token.
