🏢 ABOUT WAITLINK

Eliminating Walk-Away Loss for Walk-In Businesses.

WaitLink was built to help barbershops, medical clinics, salons, and cafes keep their waiting rooms calm, retain peak rush customers, and modernize front desk operations with zero friction.

Our Core Mission

Walk-in businesses lose up to 30% of peak rush revenue simply because waiting customers look at a crowded lobby and walk away without checking in. Traditional queue management systems require cumbersome app downloads, SMS sign-ups, or costly hardware.

WaitLink solves this with a 1-tap web check-in. Customers scan a window QR poster, instantly see their line position on their phone browser, and relax nearby until called.

Engineered for Every Stakeholder

WaitLink provides tailored, zero-friction workflows and airtight permission boundaries for guests, receptionists, and business owners.

🚶‍♂️
Walk-In Guest

Zero-App Live Ticket

Customers scan the storefront QR with their native camera. No account, password, or app store download required. They monitor live wait times on their phone and receive an automated "Your turn is ready!" SMS alert.

💻
Front Desk Staff

1-Tap Desk Console

Front desk operators and receptionists log in via 4-digit Station PIN or 1-Click Station URL. Call next, seat guests, mark completions, or add offline walk-ins in seconds, with zero access to Stripe billing or owner credentials.

👑
Shop Owner

Total Store Control

Complete administrative governance over custom branding, operating hours, staff PIN management, SMS message templates, CCPA/GDPR retention lifecycles, and multi-location performance analytics.
