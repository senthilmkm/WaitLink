JumpIn Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, accurate answers for visitors, drivers, and riders using JumpIn community carpool boards.

👋 Getting Started (Visitors)

What is JumpIn, and do I need to download an app or create an account?

JumpIn is a lightweight community carpool board built for neighbors, commuters, and local groups. No app download, password, or account signup is required for drivers or riders.

You simply enter your name and a valid 10-digit US/Canada mobile number when posting a ride or reserving a seat, and JumpIn texts you a private management link.

How do neighborhood boards (like ?g=your-group) work?

Each community group has its own isolated board URL (for example, waitlink.app/?s=jumpin&g=your-group). Rides, seats, and SMS updates on one board stay strictly inside that group.

Why does a board say "Members Only" when I try to post or reserve?

Some neighborhood groups enable Members Only mode so only pre-approved community phone numbers can post rides or reserve seats. If you see that notice, contact the group organizer shown in the popup to have your number added, or make sure you opened your group's exact ?g= link.

Does JumpIn cost money or process payments?

JumpIn itself is a free community coordination tool and does not process payments. If a driver lists an optional per-seat cost (e.g. $5 / seat (direct)), any cost-sharing is arranged directly between driver and rider.

Can I install JumpIn as an app on my iPhone or Android home screen?

Yes! JumpIn works right in your browser with no app-store download, and you can also add it to your home screen for a full-screen app experience (both for the Rides Board and the Organizer Admin Console).

• Android (Chrome): Tap the 📲 Add to Home Screen button when shown (or tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen).
• iPhone / iPad (Safari): Tap 📲 Add to Home Screen for quick steps, or tap the Share (⬆️) button at the bottom and choose Add to Home Screen.

Can I start a private JumpIn board for my own neighborhood or group?

Yes! Tap Start a Group in the top menu to request a private ?g= board for your community, manage approved members, and customize group settings.

🚗 For Drivers

How do I post a ride, and can I post for past dates?

Tap Post a ride, enter your name, 10-digit US/Canada phone number, pickup/destination, date & time, available seats (up to the group limit, max 6), estimated duration, and optional ride cost or pickup note.

You cannot post a ride for a past date or time, or beyond the group's advance booking window (30 days by default).

How do I manage my trip, confirm riders, add a passenger, or cancel?

Right after posting, your browser saves your driver session and JumpIn texts you a private view=trip manage link. From that page you can:

• Share on WhatsApp / Copy link to post your ride in group chats.
• Confirm riders (🔔 → ✓): when a rider joins, a 🔔 button appears next to their name — tap it to mark them seen (✓), which also updates their status to ✓ Confirmed.
• Add passenger manually (before departure, while seats remain; driver-added passengers start as ✓).
• Message passengers to text all active riders at once.
• Cancel trip (notifies all active riders by SMS).

Do I get an SMS when a rider joins my trip?

JumpIn updates Trip Updates live, reduces available seats immediately, and highlights the new rider with a 🔔 icon on your trip card without sending an extra SMS for each booking. You do receive an SMS whenever a rider sends you a message.

When can I mark a trip as Completed, and what happens if nobody joins?

Once the departure time has passed and at least 1 active rider is on the trip, the Complete trip button appears. If you don't tap it manually, JumpIn automatically closes the trip 1 hour after departure + estimated duration: trips with 1+ riders are marked Completed (and send an optional thank-you SMS if enabled by the group), while trips with 0 riders are automatically marked Cancelled with no thank-you SMS.

What if I open my trip in another browser (like WhatsApp) or lose my link?

Your driver access is saved in the browser you used to post and in the private link texted to your phone. If you switch browsers or lose the text, open the ride page, tap Driver? Manage this trip, and enter the phone number used to post the ride to receive a fresh manage link.

💺 For Riders

How do I reserve a seat (or multiple seats for family)?

Open any open trip with available seats, tap Reserve a seat, enter your name, 10-digit US/Canada phone number, number of seats needed, and an optional drop-off note. JumpIn reserves your seat(s) immediately and texts you a private view=seat manage link.

How do I know when the driver has seen and confirmed my seat?

Right after you reserve, your name under Passengers shows a ⏳ Pending badge. As soon as the driver opens the trip and taps 🔔 next to your name, your status updates live to ✓ Confirmed.

What if I open the trip from another browser or lose my SMS link?

Open the trip page, tap Rider? Manage seat or message driver, and enter the phone number you used when reserving to have a fresh manage link texted to you.

How do I change my seat count or cancel my reservation?

Open your trip using the same browser or your texted view=seat link before the trip departs. You can update your seat count (if seats are available) or tap Cancel my seat to release your seats back to the board immediately.

Once the departure time has passed, seat edits and cancellations are locked.

How do I message the driver before or during the ride?

On your trip page, use the quick chips (On my way, At pickup spot, 5 min late) or type a custom message and tap Send. Your message is posted to Trip Updates and texted directly to the driver.

Messaging works pre-departure and mid-trip up until departure + estimated duration + 1 hour.

When does my SMS manage link expire?

• Edit or cancel seat: valid until the trip's departure time.
• Message the driver: valid until departure + estimated duration + 1 hour.
• Replaced or ended: your old link stops working immediately if you cancel your seat, request a new link via Text me a link, or the trip is completed/cancelled.
• After expiry: completed or cancelled trips stay read-only for 7 days with all action buttons hidden, and are then purged automatically.

🔒 Privacy & SMS

Is my phone number visible to other riders or public visitors?

No. Public trip pages only show names (first/display name), pickup/drop-off notes, seat counts, and Trip Updates — never full phone numbers.

How do I stop receiving SMS messages from JumpIn?

Reply STOP to any JumpIn text message at any time to opt out of SMS notifications. (Note: you will still be able to view public trip boards in your browser.)
