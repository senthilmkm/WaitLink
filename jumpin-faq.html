JumpIn Frequently Asked Questions

Everything drivers and riders need to know about coordinating community rides on JumpIn.

Is JumpIn free to use?

Yes. JumpIn is a free community carpool coordination tool with zero booking fees and no account signup required.

How do ride costs and payments work?

WaitLink never collects fares or processes payments online. When posting a ride, drivers may optionally display a suggested per-seat cost; any cost sharing is settled offline directly between the driver and riders.

Can I reserve more than one seat?

Yes. When reserving a seat, you can choose how many seats you need (up to the seats remaining, max 6) and optionally enter a drop-off location.

How do I update or cancel my seat or trip?

JumpIn texts you a private manage link right after you post a trip or reserve a seat. Open that link anytime to update seat count, message the driver/passengers, or cancel.

Can a driver add a passenger manually?

Yes. From the driver's trip screen, tapping Add passenger lets the driver book seats for a rider and send them an SMS manage link, while updating the available seat count automatically.

Who can post or reserve rides?

Depending on community settings, trips may be open to all visitors, require admin approval, or be restricted to approved community members managed by the JumpIn administrator.

How do I stop SMS messages?

Reply STOP to any JumpIn text message at any time to opt out of SMS alerts.
